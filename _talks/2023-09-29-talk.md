## Overview

At the Detroit Observatory’s Mid-Autumn Festival, I introduced the Chang’e lunar exploration program and the step-by-step progression from lunar orbiters and landers to sample return. The talk focused on Chang’e 5, which returned about 1.7 kilograms of lunar material to Earth in 2020, and explained why these comparatively young samples are scientifically valuable for understanding the Moon’s volcanic and impact history.
