## Overview

This presentation explored the vertical structure of the AU Microscopii debris disk by jointly modeling JWST/NIRCam F444W scattered-light images and ALMA Band 6 interferometric visibilities. The two datasets trace different grain populations, so their combination provides a way to test whether small and large grains share the same spatial distribution.

The model uses dataset-specific likelihoods with a shared geometric framework and Bayesian inference to estimate the disk’s vertical scale height. Preliminary modeling suggests that the small grains traced by JWST may be more vertically extended than the larger grains traced by ALMA, consistent with grain-size-dependent disk dynamics.
