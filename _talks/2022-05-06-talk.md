## Overview

I co-presented this evening observational session for Astro 1 at UC Santa Barbara with Professor Robert Antonucci. Beneath the campus sky, we guided students through major constellations, the life cycles of prominent stars, and the planets visible to the naked eye, connecting classroom concepts with direct observation.
