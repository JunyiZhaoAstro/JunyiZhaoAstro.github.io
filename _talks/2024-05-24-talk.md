## Overview

We tested photometric methods for identifying O-type and early B-type emission-line (OBe) stars in the Large Magellanic Cloud. Continuum-subtracted Hα photometry alone produced substantial contamination, while combining Hα excess with broadband colors improved the selection of hot-star candidates.

We then used Gaia DR3 proper motions to compare the kinematics of candidate OBe populations in the Large and Small Magellanic Clouds. The project provided an initial framework for connecting emission-line selection with the dynamical histories of massive stars.
