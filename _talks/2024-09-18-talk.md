## Overview

At the Detroit Observatory’s Mid-Autumn Festival, I traced the Chang’e program from its early orbiters and landers through the Chang’e 5 near-side sample return. I then introduced the scientific and engineering challenges of exploring the lunar far side, where direct radio communication with Earth is impossible.

The talk concluded with Chang’e 6, which returned the first samples collected from the Moon’s far side in 2024, and discussed how these materials can help reveal why the Moon’s two hemispheres evolved so differently.
