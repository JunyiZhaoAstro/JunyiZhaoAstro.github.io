## Overview

MWC 758 is a young Herbig Ae star surrounded by a structured protoplanetary disk with prominent spiral arms. We searched JWST/NIRCam coronagraphic observations for a proposed embedded protoplanet using reference-star differential imaging, angular differential imaging, and forward modeling.

We did not recover a statistically significant point source at the candidate location. Our analysis placed contrast limits of approximately 10⁻⁵ in the available bands and showed that residual disk structure remains an important source of confusion. Deeper observations, particularly near F430M, would help distinguish a faint companion from scattered light and thermal emission in the disk.
