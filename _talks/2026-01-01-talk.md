## Overview

At the 247th Meeting of the American Astronomical Society, I presented a multiwavelength study of the AU Microscopii debris disk’s vertical structure. The analysis jointly models JWST/NIRCam F444W scattered-light images and ALMA Band 6 interferometric visibilities within a shared geometric framework.

Because the two datasets trace different grain populations, the joint model tests whether small and large grains have different vertical distributions. Preliminary results suggest that the small grains traced by JWST may be more vertically extended than the larger grains traced by ALMA, providing a new constraint on grain-size-dependent disk dynamics.
